Privacy Policy: Geococcyx

Geococcyx runs only on its owner's own watch and is used only by its owner.

  • It uses Google Drive access solely to save the owner's run files (TCX) to a folder it creates in the owner's Google Drive.
  • It cannot see any other Drive files, and it does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any data with anyone.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's watch and computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
